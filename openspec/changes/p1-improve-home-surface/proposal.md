- Home's timesheet row is only "GS 2 · Polity & Governance · 2h 1m" — the topic ("Laxmikanth — Fundamental Rights, DPSP, Parliament") is gone; Study truncates it to "Laxmikanth — Fundamental Righ…".

- The rank pill clips ("The Novice Aspi…").
- Answer-writing ritual is a bare 1/2/3 count — no question, no time, no self-score. The newspaper checkbox works but the editorial has nowhere to go.

## What Changes
- Home timesheet rows show the topic under the subject line (up to 2 lines, full text). Study screen shows the full topic, untruncated.
- Share card uses human names: her name and partner name from settings, subject as "Polity & Governance (GS2)", plus the topic line.
- Rank pill never clips (shorten labels or let it wrap).
- **Answer-writing chips open a micro-sheet**: question/source, minutes, optional self-rating (0–10) and note — saved as a mains practice record visible in Tests mains history.
- **Newspaper ritual**: after checking, an optional one-line note ("Editorial: …") is stored with the day and shown beside the struck-through row.
