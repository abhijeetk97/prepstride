## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The share card SHALL use human names and the topic
The share card SHALL label the subject as "Polity & Governance (GS2)"-style (subject name with paper), include the day's topic line, and use her name and the partner's name from settings.

#### Scenario: Share after a Polity morning
- **WHEN** the user opens the share card after logging a GS2 session with topic and reading the paper
- **THEN** the card reads with the full subject label, the topic, her name, and the partner's name — never bare "GS2" or the literal word "Partner"
